Privacy Policy

Pixogle Studio · operated by Pixogle Ltd · last updated 27 October 2026

Pixogle Studio is an internal content-management tool. It is used only by Pixogle to manage Instagram professional accounts that Pixogle owns and operates. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When an account owner connects an Instagram professional account they own, the app may access, through the Instagram API:

  • the account's basic profile information (username, account id);
  • the account's own posts and their insights (views, reach, likes, saves, shares);
  • comments left by other people on the account's own posts, including the commenter's username and the comment text.

The app does not access other accounts' posts, private messages, or any data about people beyond the comments they choose to leave on the connected account's own posts.

How the data is used

  • To publish the account owner's own posts.
  • To show the account owner how their posts perform.
  • To show comments in one place and to post replies to them from the account. Reply suggestions may be drafted with the help of an AI service; comment text is sent to that service only to draft a reply.

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone for advertising or any other purpose, and we do not use it to build profiles of the people who comment.

Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored on equipment controlled by Pixogle. Comments and replies are kept for up to 12 months so that the same comment is not answered twice, then deleted. Post insights are kept for as long as the account owner uses the tool.

Your choices

If you commented on one of our accounts' posts and want your comment data removed from our tool, see our data deletion instructions or contact us at the address below. Deleting a comment on Instagram itself is done in the Instagram app.
