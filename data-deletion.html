Data Deletion Instructions

Pixogle Studio · operated by Pixogle · last updated 27 October 2026

Pixogle Studio is an internal tool that Pixogle uses to manage its own Instagram accounts. The only personal data it holds about other people is comments they left on those accounts' posts (username and comment text), plus any reply we posted.

To have your data deleted

  1. Email info@pixogle.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Include your Instagram username, so we can find your comments.
  3. We delete every comment and reply record linked to that username from our tool within 30 days, and email you to confirm.

Account owners

An account owner who disconnects an Instagram account from Pixogle Studio (or removes the app in Instagram under Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites) stops all further access at once. The data already stored for that account is deleted within 30 days of a request to the address above.

Comments themselves live on Instagram; deleting a comment there is done in the Instagram app.
